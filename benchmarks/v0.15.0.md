# Benchmarks - v0.15.0

Date: 2026-05-08
Platform: macOS 26.4.1 (Apple Silicon, arm64)
Rust: rustc 1.95.0 (59807616e 2026-04-14)
Command: `cargo bench`

Comparisons reference the last checked-in benchmark note, v0.14.0. v0.15.0
adds configurable popup widths and an opt-in adjacent description box (#116);
the changes are confined to `gc-overlay` rendering and `gc-pty` handler
plumbing, so none of the suggestion-engine, parser, or spec-loading hot paths
are expected to move.

Gnuplot was not installed; Criterion used the plotters backend.

## Summary

`cargo bench` completed successfully. Every benchmark group is flat or
improved relative to v0.14.0. Most deltas land in the -1% to -7% range, which
is consistent with run-to-run variance and a cooler machine rather than
release-driven gains — `gc-suggest` and `gc-parser` were not touched.

The keystroke path remains comfortably under target: `engine_suggest_sync`
command-position and subcommand cases stay under 18 us, alias and filesystem
paths stay near 1 ms, and `priority_sort` 10k cases stay under 1 ms.

`memory/embedded_specs_heap_walk` continues to recover: 9.76 ms vs 10.45 ms
in v0.14.0 (-6.58%). It is still elevated against the v0.12.3 baseline of
4.94 ms; nothing in this release touches embedded specs, so the trend is
worth keeping under observation in the next run.
